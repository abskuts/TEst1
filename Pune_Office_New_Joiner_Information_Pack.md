# Pune Office: New Joiner First-Day Information Pack

Welcome to the Pune office. This guide explains what to do before and when you arrive on your first day.

## 1. Office location

**Office:** WeWork, 3rd Floor, AtkinsRéalis, Eleven West  
**Address:** Baner, Pune

Please plan your journey in advance and allow extra time for traffic, building entry, and parking arrangements.

## 2. Before you arrive

- **Call Hira Dhanavat before arriving at the office.**
- Hira will guide you on the **first-day parking arrangements**.
- Parking instructions are provided for your first day only; please follow any subsequent guidance shared by the administration team.

## 3. Where to meet

On arrival, please go to the **3rd-floor lobby sitting area** at WeWork.

**Meeting point:** 3rd-floor lobby sitting area  
**Who will receive you:** Hira Dhanavat

Please wait at the meeting point until Hira meets you and guides you through the next steps.

## 4. Your points of contact

| Contact | Role | How they can help |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hira Dhanavat** | Administration point of contact | First-day arrival, parking guidance, office access, and general office-related support. |
| **Your Talent Acquisition Partner (Buddy)** | Buddy | First-day support, introductions, onboarding questions, and help settling in. Your Talent Acquisition Partner will be shared with you separately. |

## 5. First-day checklist

Before leaving for the office, please ensure that you:

- Have called Hira Dhanavat for parking guidance.
- Have planned to arrive at the 3rd-floor lobby sitting area.
- Have your mobile phone available so Hira or your Buddy can reach you if needed.
- Have any joining documents or identification requested by the Talent Acquisition team.

## 6. If you need help

If you are delayed, cannot find the building, or need assistance on arrival, contact **Hira Dhanavat** first. For onboarding or joining-related questions, contact your **Talent Acquisition Partner (Buddy)**.

We look forward to welcoming you to the Pune office.
